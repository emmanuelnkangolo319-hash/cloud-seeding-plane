# Cloud-seeding-plane
A rc plane that deposits 150g of salt into clouds to increase chances of rain.

What this plane can do:

-Collect data of the temperature, atmospheric pressure, height and humidity of the air. This data will be used to see how many trips are needed to give the cloud a chance of rain or literally any type of precipitation.

-Take photos and live videos.

-Deposit a maximum of 150g of salt into a cloud.


I made this mainly because i think that Weather Report(from Jo Jos) has one of the coolest powers, and i thought how useful it could be, not for combat, but to help people. I thought that this could be in so many farms, to avoid dry seasons entirely, i believe that humanity is to advanced to keep worrying about food, so im trying to push us in the right direction.
