# Cloud-seeding-plane
A rc plane that deposits 150g of salt into clouds to increase chances of rain.

What this plane can do:
-Collect data of the temperature, atmospheric pressure, height and humidity of the air. Thia data will be used to see how many trips are needed to give the cloud a chance of rain or literally any type of precipitation.
-Take photos and live videos.
-Deposit a maximum of 150g of salt into a cloud.
